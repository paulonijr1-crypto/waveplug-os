# The October brush launch

🔒 **Confidential.** JR found Torino Pro's supplier, customised the bristles to his own spec, and secured colours Torino does not have. Nobody else in the niche knows this. It never appears in an ad, a caption, an email, a customer reply or a script. Say "our own brushes, made to our spec" and stop there.

This is the biggest revenue event in the brand's history. It is also the easiest one to waste.

---

## The situation

Torino Pro has had a monopoly in wave brushes for years and is the default recommendation in every wave conversation online. JR is about to release a directly competitive product at a spec he controls, in colours that do not otherwise exist, to an audience of 190k people who already trust him specifically on brushes.

That is a genuinely rare position. The risk is not the product. **The risk is dropping it into a store that loses 82% of the people who reach the checkout.**

## Why the waitlist is the most important item on the list

JR's email list **has never been sent a single campaign.** Not one, in two and a half years. The flows make about £222 a month against £2.4k of revenue, roughly 10%, where a healthy DTC brand runs 25 to 35%.

A launch to a cold list is a launch to nobody. A launch to a list that has been hearing from JR twice a week for a month, that opted in specifically because they want these brushes, is a different event entirely.

**Open the waitlist now, before the product is ready.** Every TikTok from here points at it. Six weeks of collection at JR's traffic levels builds a list with a genuine reason to open, and that list is what the launch actually goes to.

This is the highest leverage thing available and it costs a page and a form.

## The sequence

### Now to launch minus 3 weeks
- Architect: checkout, currency, express payments. **Nothing else matters more than this.**
- Architect: waitlist page live.
- Postman: fix deliverability first, then rewrite the abandoned checkout flow. That flow is what catches the 488 people a quarter who walk, and it currently converts 1.2%.
- Voice: every video ends pointing at the waitlist.
- Chemist: confirm the launch bundle and work out **how many butters and pomades JR can physically make.** That number is the real cap on launch day, not the brush count.

### Launch minus 3 weeks
- Voice: seed brushes to other wave creators for honest testimonials. Their videos land in launch week, not before.
- Postman: warm the list, two emails a week, useful content not teasers.
- Scout: lock two evergreen sound beds for the launch and reveal videos. Do not let the launch be hostage to whatever happens to be trending that week.

### Launch minus 1 week
- Voice: teaser. Show the brush without explaining it.
- Postman: launch sequence drafted and built, JR pastes the copy since the API cannot write flow content.
- Ledger: baseline reading so the lift is actually measurable afterwards.

### Launch week
- Email the waitlist first, before any public post. They opted in, they get it first, and that is what makes the next waitlist work.
- Voice: the brushes appear in every wash and style from now on. Every video is a soft demo forever.
- Closer: retarget the waitlist and video viewers only. No cold acquisition, the warm audience converts far better over a short window.
- Chemist: watch stock daily. Decide the oversell policy **before** launch night, not at 2am during it.

---

## What must not happen

- **Do not launch into the current checkout.** If the funnel is unfixed on the day the brushes land, the single biggest moment this brand will get is spent proving that 8 out of 10 buyers cannot complete an order.
- **Do not name the supplier.** Not in a caption, not to a creator being seeded, not in a reply to a customer asking who makes them.
- **Do not let the launch depend on a trending sound.** Bank the beds in September.
- **Do not oversell the brushes** to protect a launch day number. This audience's entire relationship with JR is trust, and a two week silence after a delayed order costs more than the orders were worth.
- **Do not let it be the only thing that ships.** Retention is the compounding prize. Subscribe and save on the butter and pomade, put in before the launch, turns a spike into a monthly floor.

---

## After it lands

The brushes are a moment. The business is what the moment converts into.

- Every brush buyer is a butter and pomade customer who has not bought yet. The post purchase flow decides whether that happens.
- 21.4% of customers come back, on consumables, where 35 to 45% is normal. The brush launch is the largest injection of new customers this brand will get, and whether they come back is decided by what The Postman built before they arrived, not after.
- Ledger reads it at 14 and 30 days. Not launch day. Launch day numbers tell you about the launch, not about the business.
