## THE ONE NUMBER THAT MATTERS

Last 90 days, the funnel:

```
11,400 sessions
     ↓  7.3%
   835 added to cart
     ↓  77.8%
   650 reached checkout
     ↓  18.5%   ← THIS
   120 completed
```

**530 people reached your checkout in 90 days and did not buy.**

They wanted it. They had their card out. They got to the last screen and left. A healthy store completes 45% to 65% of checkouts. You are at 18.5%, and it has been that low every single month for a year.

That is not a content problem or a traffic problem. Something at your checkout is breaking.

**What it is worth:** get to 40% completion and that is +140 orders per quarter, about **+£2,567 a month**, with zero extra traffic, zero extra content, zero ad spend. It roughly doubles the business on its own.

**Most likely causes, in order:**

1. **You are a GBP store and 73% of your traffic is American.** US shoppers see £ signs, do not know what they are actually paying, and their bank adds a foreign transaction fee at the end. Proof: US mobile converts at **0.94%**, UK mobile converts at **2.43%**. Same products, same site, same content. The only difference is that Americans are being asked to buy in a foreign currency.
2. **Shipping cost revealed at the final step.** Classic checkout killer.
3. **No express payment options.** If Shop Pay, PayPal and Apple Pay are not on, a mobile buyer has to type a full card number. 96% of your traffic is mobile.

## THE FIVE MOVES, RANKED

### 1. Fix the checkout. (Biggest, cheapest, fastest)
**Worth: +£2,000 to £3,000 a month**

- Turn on **Shopify Markets** and set the US to show USD with local pricing. It is free on your Basic plan.
- Turn on **Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay**.
- Put shipping cost on the product page, not the last screen. Or bake it into the price and say free shipping.
- Kill any forced account creation. Guest checkout only.
- Then go through your own checkout on your phone, as an American, using a VPN, and buy something. You will find it in about 90 seconds.

### 2. Give the American market its own store.
**Worth: +£760 to £1,990 a month**

7,436 US mobile sessions last quarter converting at 0.94%. If they converted like your UK visitors do, that is 178 orders instead of 70. Same traffic.

Beyond currency: US delivery time and cost need to be stated up front, in days, in dollars.

### 3. Turn email on. It is currently switched off.
**Worth: +£400 to £800 a month now, and it scales with everything else**

Right now every email flow you have made **£667 in 90 days**. That is £222 a month, 10% of revenue. Good DTC brands run 25% to 35%.

What is actually wrong:

- **Deliverability is broken.** Your flows are delivering at 85% to 90%. It should be 98%+. You are burning 1 in 8 emails before anyone sees them. Fix the sending domain first, nothing else in email matters until this is done.
- **Abandoned checkout flow sent to 385 people and got 5 sales.** 1.2% conversion, 0.6% click rate. The copy is generic app-default text. This is the flow that catches the 530 people from problem #1, and it is not catching them.
- **You have never sent a single campaign.** The whole list has never heard from you.
- Open rates are 13% to 27%. The welcome flow converts at 2.7% and makes £2.43 per recipient, which proves people do want to hear from you when the email is actually about something.

The Postman agent already exists for this. Point it at deliverability first, then the abandoned checkout copy, then the first campaign.

### 4. Lift AOV from £60 to £75.
**Worth: +25% on every order you already get**

- Free shipping over £75. Right now nothing pushes anyone past £60.
- Post purchase one click upsell. Someone buys the butter, offer the pomade at 20% off on the thank you page. No new traffic, pure margin.
- Bundle prompt in the cart: "add the soft brush for £12 and finish the pattern."
- Your Starter Pack is 47% of revenue from about a quarter of orders. More people should be landing on it. It should be the first thing on the homepage, not one product among many.

### 5. Retention, because these are consumables.
**Worth: compounding**

21.4% of your customers come back. For butters, pomades and shampoo bars that should be 35% to 45%. People run out. They just do not get reminded.

- **Subscribe and save on the Moisturising Butter and Pomade.** 10% off, ships every 6 or 8 weeks. That turns one time buyers into predictable monthly revenue and it is the single strongest thing you can do before the brush launch.
- Replenishment email timed to when the jar actually runs out.

## THE OCTOBER BRUSH LAUNCH

The brushes are the biggest single revenue event in the brand's history and they land in about six weeks.

Do not launch them into the store you have now. If they drop while checkout is losing 8 out of 10 buyers and the email list has never been warmed, you burn the moment.

**Between now and October:**

1. Fix checkout and currency. (Weeks 1 to 2)
2. Fix email deliverability and rewrite the flows. (Weeks 2 to 3)
3. **Open a waitlist now.** Every TikTok from here sends people to a waitlist page. You are collecting emails for six weeks and building a list that has a reason to open. That list is what you launch to.
4. Warm the list with 2 emails a week from September so they are not hearing from you for the first time on launch day.

## THE PATH TO £20K A MONTH

Today: about 4,800 sessions × 1.05% × £60 = £3,000 a month.

Target: **10,000 sessions × 2.5% × £80 = £20,000 a month.**

That is 2x traffic, 2.4x conversion, 1.3x AOV. None of those three is a stretch on its own. The 2.5% conversion is not aspirational, your UK mobile traffic already converts at 2.43% today.

**And note the order.** Conversion and AOV come first, traffic last. Doubling traffic into a checkout that loses 81% of buyers just wastes twice as many people.
