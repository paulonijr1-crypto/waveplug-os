# The Wave Plug — Website Audit
**Prepared for:** Paul (JR The Wave Plug) · **Date:** August 2026 · **Store:** thewaveplug.com (Shopify Basic, GBP)

---

## 1. Executive summary

The Wave Plug has the two hardest things to build already in place: **a real audience (190k on TikTok) and genuine product credibility (4.86★ across 41 reviews).** The problem isn't demand — it's that the website leaks most of the traffic your content sends it. You convert visitors at **~1.0%** when a store with this much warm traffic should be doing 2–3%+.

The money is being lost in two specific places:

1. **Checkout** — 651 people reached checkout in 90 days, only 128 finished. That's ~20% completion vs. a healthy 40–60%. This is the single biggest leak.
2. **Social traffic converts at 0.35%** — your biggest brand asset (TikTok/IG) sends the worst-converting visitors, which points at the *landing experience*, not the audience.

Fix the funnel and the same traffic you already have could roughly double revenue without a single extra follower. This document ranks exactly what to fix, in what order.

## 3. The funnel — where the money leaks

Last 90 days, out of 12,494 sessions:

- **875 added to cart** (7% of sessions) — *the storefront/PDPs aren't convincing enough visitors to try.*
- **651 reached checkout** (74% of carts) — cart → checkout is fine.
- **128 completed** (20% of checkouts) — **massive drop. This is the priority-one leak.**

Two leaks, two different fixes: **(A)** get more visitors to add to cart (product pages, photos, trust), and **(B)** stop the bleed at checkout.

---

## 4. Prioritized fixes (impact vs. effort)

### 🔴 CRITICAL — do first

**1. Diagnose and fix the 20% checkout completion.**
Shipping is free worldwide, so cost-shock isn't the cause. The likely culprits, in order:
- **Currency.** 67% of revenue is US, but the store charges in GBP. US buyers see "£" and either hesitate or bounce at the final step. **Fix:** turn on Shopify Markets with local currency display (USD for US, etc.) so prices feel native.
- **Slow dispatch shown at checkout.** Your policy is "processed within 5–10 business days" *before* international shipping time — that's slow for a US buyer used to 2-day delivery, and if it surfaces at checkout it kills momentum. **Fix:** tighten dispatch and/or reframe (e.g. "ships in 48 hours").
- **Trust at the final step.** No visible guarantee, unclear delivery date, unfamiliar brand for a first-time US buyer. **Fix:** add reassurance (guarantee, delivery estimate, secure-checkout cues).
- **Action:** run a live test checkout as a US customer to see exactly what they see. (I'll need your go-ahead before changing any settings.)

**2. Fix the GBP→local currency experience site-wide** (same root cause as above, but affects browsing too — people price-shop in a foreign currency and leave before cart).

### 🟠 HIGH — big impact, moderate effort

**3. Rewrite the Elite Starter Pack page (£130, ~43% of revenue).**
Currently the description is a bare ALL-CAPS list of item names. For your single most important product that's leaving money on the table. Needs:
- Benefit-led copy in your voice (what the *results* are, not just the contents).
- **Value framing:** "£130 for what would cost £X bought separately."
- Individual photos of everything inside + a lifestyle shot.
- The 4C story woven in — this is the product that delivers your whole mission.

**4. Rescue social traffic (0.35% conversion).**
Your TikTok/IG send huge volume that barely converts. Likely a message-match problem — the content promises one thing, the landing page shows a generic homepage. **Fix:** dedicated landing pages that match what a video promised, and make sure the first thing a phone visitor sees is the hero product + proof, not a menu.

**5. Surface social proof harder.** 4.86★/41 reviews is a real asset but it's buried at the bottom. Move star ratings up near the top of PDPs and the homepage hero, add review photos, and feature the strongest quotes.

### 🟢 QUICK WINS — low effort, do this week

**6. Fix the dead social links.** Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok icons in the header and footer all link to `#` (nowhere). For a brand built on social this is a genuine miss — point them to @jnr.certi and @jnrstillwavyy.

**7. Stop featuring sold-out products.** "Triple Threat" shows Sold Out in the homepage Best Sellers. Either restock, hide it, or add a back-in-stock email capture.

**8. Fix the "Track Your Order" link.** It sends people to a different domain (waveplug.shop) — jarring and a small trust wobble. Keep tracking on-brand/on-domain.

**9. Make the email capture obvious.** You advertise "10% off for new subscribers" but there's no clear signup. A simple popup/embed here starts feeding the Klaviyo list (which is currently underused).

### 🟡 MEDIUM — after the above

**10. Retention system.** 25% return rate + mostly one-time buyers = your biggest untapped lever given products are consumable (butters, oils, bars run out). Klaviyo flows (post-purchase, replenishment reminder, win-back) turn one-time buyers into repeat. → future Email Agent.

**11. Product photography pass.** You flagged this yourself. Consistent, premium, mobile-first shots across the catalog — especially the hero bundle and the coming brushes.

**12. Homepage narrative.** Lead with the 4C founder story sooner — it's your strongest differentiator and no competitor can copy it. Right now it sits below the fold.
