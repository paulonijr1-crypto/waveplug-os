# Brush Launch, Behind the Scenes
_Romanticised day in the life for the January 2027 brush launch · By The Voice for JR · drafted 2026-11-02 · about 50s, voiceover led_

## The day, in JR's words
Up at 8, drop off orders, pharmacy till 2, get a couple of shots up before it gets dark, come back, make more products, brush, pack the orders, get ready to do it all again. "I love my life."

3. The drop off, from outside. Bag in, walk out.
4. Pharmacy from outside only, or the lanyard going on in the car. Never inside.
5. Two o'clock walk out, the light already low.
6. The shoot. Hands, the tripod, golden light. If the brushes are in frame keep them out of focus or in silhouette, no colours, no bristles, until the drop.

8. The mirror. Brush in hand, the crown, this is the longest held shot.
9. Tape, label, box. City and state visible, never the name.
10. Boxes lined up by the door, light off. Cut to black on the closer.
