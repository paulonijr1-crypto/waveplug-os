# B-roll bank

_Plug, 28/10/2026. Shot list for JR. B-roll is line 1 of the two line formula (see `content/STYLE-STUDY-justinescameraroll.md`). Shoot it once, write line 2 later, pull the clip. Every clip 10 to 15 seconds, vertical, no talking. Five framings every time so clips cut together across days: wide of the room, hands close, over the shoulder, walking in, the put it down shot. Lock exposure before recording in lamp light._

## The pharmacy (the shift that pays for the brush)
- Badge or lanyard on in the morning, off at six
- Shop front from outside, lights on, walking in. Different shop each time (locum), that is a story on its own
- Locking up, car park in the dark. Late October London is black by five, lamp light and headlights
- The drive home, dashboard glow, rain on the windscreen, brake lights
- NOTHING inside the dispensary. No patients, labels, screens or shelves. Ask the manager before filming indoors. Badge and door are enough

## The make (the product with your hands in it)
- Raw murumuru before the melt, the melt, the stir
- The pour, lids on one by one, the row of finished jars
- Scale with the weight showing (numbers = line 2 material)
- Labels going on, hand smoothing one down
- Steam, hob, kitchen at night with one light
- Reflection in the kitchen window while stirring

## Packing (two orders like two hundred)
- Orders screen, names covered, count visible
- Label printer running, label coming out
- US state on an address, name blurred ("this one's going to Texas")
- Box fold, tape pull, the sound
- Stack by the door, walk to Londis, parcel over the counter, receipt
- Handwritten note going in (start doing one if not)

## The comeback (day 53 on 28/10/2026)
- THE most valuable shot: same mirror, same angle, same light, every morning, five seconds. Tape mark on the floor for the phone. In three months this is the before and after nobody else has
- Dime of butter on a fingertip
- Crown isolated, brush working just that spot
- Hard, medium, soft laid out in order
- Durag on at night, off in the morning
- Kyle's chair, cape, lineup from behind, mirror reveal
- The day 1 clip, kept where it can always be found

## The brush (the January story)
- Three samples turned slowly in lamp light: green and yellow, metallic purple, metallic blue
- Bristles close, thumb across them
- Rejected sample next to the good one
- Brush on the durag it matches
- The box, customs sticker, supplier name covered (supplier stays private)

## London to a kid in Houston
- Train or bus window, estate at night, chicken shop sign, the Thames if passed, red bus, keys in the door
- The room, tripod, lamp light, phone on the stand
- Court at dusk, the ball, the walk there, one shot going in
- Gym walk in, pull ups, mirror

## The community
- Comments on screen, best one zoomed
- A DM asking "do you ship to the US", name covered
- The wheel spin for the reaction giveaway
- The 200K screen
- Customer photos, with their yes

## Cadence
One 90 minute session on a make day for product and brush shots. Then 30 seconds a day for the rest. After two weeks there is no filming for content, only writing line 2 and pulling a clip.
