# Brush line naming brainstorm

_Plug, 28/10/2026. For the Torino rival brushes dropping January 2027. Three colours shown so far: green and yellow (everyone's favourite), dark metallic purple, metallic blue. Firmness per colour not yet decided. JR's coaching: hard holds the pattern while wolfing, medium is the everyday and deepens, soft is the finisher that lays and shines._

## Avoid (already used in the niche)
- **Crown**: Crown Quality sells wave brushes
- **King / Kings**: Kings Brushes, JR named them as a competitor on camera
- **Builder**: WaveBuilder
- **Royalty**: JR's own headwear line, keep it there
- Any number system: that is Torino's language (#1040, #150, #200)

## Direction 1: The Underground (recommended)
Each colour is a London tube line. Scales forever.

| Colour | Line |
|---|---|
| Green and yellow | District |
| Dark metallic purple | Elizabeth (the royal one) |
| Metallic blue | Piccadilly |
| Future red | Central |
| Future light blue | Victoria |
| Future silver | Jubilee |
| Future black | Northern |
| Future orange | Overground |

Why: London romance for a US customer, "underground" means the real ones not the mainstream (the Torino story without saying Torino), one word per colour.
Check: TfL protects "Underground", the roundel and the map. Line names are place names in wide use but check before printing. Never use the roundel or the tube font.

## Direction 2: The jobs (JR's coaching as names)
- Hard: **The Wolf**, holds the pattern while wolfing
- Medium: **The Daily**, the everyday, deepens
- Soft: **The Finisher**, lays flat, brings the shine

Works alone or under any colour system: Piccadilly Wolf, District Daily, Elizabeth Finisher. Nobody else can name brushes this way because nobody else coaches this way.

## Direction 3: Tide
Low Tide (soft), Mid Tide (medium), High Tide (hard). Clean, calm, premium. Less story.

## Direction 4: The Prescription
The pharmacist thread, no other wave brand can touch it.
Low Dose (soft), Daily Dose (medium), Full Dose (hard). Line name "The Prescription".
Most memorable, most divisive. Say it on camera before deciding.

## Direction 5: Plain premium
"Wave Plug Pro Series" in Lime, Royal, Cobalt. Safe, sells, not talked about.

## Direction 6: The drop name (separate from product names)
**The Takeover, January 2027.** JR's own words on camera, "taking over the game with this one." For the waitlist, countdown and first video.

## Before anything is final
1. Say each name in a customer's sentence: "I brush with the Wolf", "I got the Piccadilly". Stiff in the mouth means stiff on the shelf.
2. Trademark search on the line name (UK IPO and USPTO) and the handle and domain.
3. Shortlist two, let the audience vote. That is a video (hand them the decision) and it is what Justine did when her camera series had no name.
