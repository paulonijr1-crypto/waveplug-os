## JR's original
> Come with me to get a new car. My old car broke down for the second time in 2 years, and amounted to 5k+ in maintenance a Mercedes c class. I wanted to upgrade to Mercedes cla and was this close to buying it but I had to make a business decision, with my new brush line landing in January and my plan to monopolise the brush market with moq costs, storage costs and I'm gonna need crazy funding 50k+ to be exact I had to think, do I want to look lit now or look crazy lit in 2 years. So I chose the latter and got this 2016 Mazda, temporary car just to get to work and back for the next year. I'm super confident that this is by far the most important decision in my plan to take over. If any of you guys feel like you need to impress your hometown and prove your motion with material things take from me to always see the long term goal instead of looking lit right now create a plan to look lit for life.

Rating: 8/10 substance, 6/10 structure. Fixes: twist moved to second zero, 75s cut to 45s, "monopolise" replaced with "taking over the game" (JR's phrase, matches The Takeover drop name), moral cut to one line, "work" to "the pharmacy", added "I'm not asking anyone for it".
