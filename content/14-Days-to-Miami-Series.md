# 14 Days to Miami — Viral Series Concept
_Built by The Content Plug for JR · Aug 2026_

## The big idea
**"14 Days to Miami."** A daily comeback series: JR has 2 weeks till Miami and hasn't trained in 2 months. Real deadline + honest "before" = the classic underdog arc people can't stop watching. Every video is "Day X of 14," so the countdown itself becomes the hook and the reason to follow (they want to see the Day 14 payoff).

**Why it works:** relatable start (anyone who's fallen off), a ticking clock (tension), and a guaranteed payoff (the Miami reveal). It deepens the parasocial bond — they feel like they're on the journey with you.

**Brand tie (subtle, not salesy):** the thesis is the FULL PACKAGE — Miami means physique + fresh waves + fits. Waves are part of looking your best, so The Wave Plug rides along naturally, peaking in the "waves prep" episode.

---

## Format: VOICEOVER-LED
This series is narrated in JR's voice over the footage — calm, honest, brotherly, UK slang natural. Voiceover is the sales/connection engine and sidesteps on-camera discomfort. **Keep a quiet trending sound UNDER the voice** so you don't lose all the algorithm reach. Always add on-screen captions (most watch on mute).

**Delivery notes for recording the VO:**
- Talk like you're telling a mate, not presenting. Slow, low-key, real.
- Leave small pauses — let the footage breathe.
- Punch your signature emphasis words (the CAPS below).
- End on your closer every time: "and as always, stay wavy man."
