## 🏆 Winning hooks
_(dated, with why they worked)_
- 2026-08-02 (onboarding): Signature emphasis lines that land — "I'm ACTUALLY thinning", "I'm switching my ENTIRE pattern without SCALPING". Caps = punched words. Reuse this cadence.
- 2026-08-02: **JR loves HONEST, real-life minimal hooks** — e.g. "didn't even get to brush my hair today." Relatable imperfection + a real stake (any waver knows skipping a day matters) stops the scroll with zero clickbait. Lean into authenticity/chaos as hooks. On messy days, keep the wave thread alive via "durag stays on to protect the swirl" instead of forcing a polished wave moment. **Tone on these = tired/human**: convey the longing to brush through the honest fact + exhaustion ("didn't even get to brush today, the drive took the life out of me") — but DON'T say the word "wish." Imply it, don't state it. Not defiant flexing; the vulnerability is what makes it relatable.
- **HOOK RULE 2026-08-02:** Never open a VO with filler ("real talk", "so", "okay so", "guys"). The **first ~5 spoken words must be stimulating** — tension, stakes or intrigue straight away (e.g. "Five hours in the car nearly finished me"). The opener is the most valuable real estate in the video. Good openers JR likes: "the state I'm in right now…", "five hours nearly finished me". He also likes the playful-confident framing **"quick day in the life of a superstar"** — self-aware, on-brand with his journey-to-greatness identity.
- **STYLE 2026-08-02:** Don't END scripts by recapping/listing everything that happened — the close should be clean and forward ("some days are just like that, you keep it moving"), never a summary of the day.
- **STYLE 2026-08-02:** On-screen text ONLY for the first ~5 seconds (the hook). NO on-screen text after that — voiceover + visuals carry the rest.
