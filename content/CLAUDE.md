- **Reliable reach format: "Wash & Style" set to trending music.** JR's theory (sound): pairing already-satisfying wash & style visuals with a viral song makes the algorithm push it to people who want the song regardless. Historically his biggest winner.
  - ⚠️ **Recently underperforming.** Likely format fatigue (overused) or execution drift — not a dead format. Fix by rotating hooks/angles and riding the pattern-shift series for fresh narrative, rather than abandoning it.
- **Talking videos get fewer views but drive more SALES.** They build trust and convert. His storytelling is the ceiling — lifting it lifts both views and sales.
- **Why people follow:** the wash & style is satisfying enough that even non-wavers follow for the ASMR/satisfaction; for actual wavers, JR is a community leader (almost nobody else consistently makes quality waves content).
- **Strategic read:** use Wash & Style (music) for REACH / top-of-funnel, talking/voiceover videos for CONVERSION. A healthy week needs BOTH — don't over-index on either.
