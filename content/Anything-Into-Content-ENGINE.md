# Anything into Content ENGINE
_By The Voice for JR · One real line in, one film ready script out. Sister file to `Day-in-the-Life-ENGINE.md`, which is this same converter pointed at a single day._

## The idea in three lines
Content is not something you go looking for. It is what you already did today, run through five questions. The day in the life engine proved it, one line about a five hour drive and a broken freezer became a script. This file makes the same thing work for everything else. A comment, a customer, an order, a batch of butter, a mistake, a win.

## The two tests
A moment only has to pass two tests before it counts.
1. **Is it real?** It happened or it is happening. A fact from your day, not an idea for a video.
2. **Is there a wave thread?** Either waves are the subject, or waves ride along. Durag on, brush in the bag, the swirl update, the brushes in your hand. If there is no thread, do not force one. Make it a story post and let the follow be the CTA.

Pass both and it is content. Everything below is just the conversion.

## The converter, five questions, thirty seconds
Run them in order. Each answer feeds the next.

| # | Question | What it decides | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What actually happened?** One honest line. | The raw material | "Someone in Texas asked if we ship to the US" |
| 2 | **Where is the stake a waver feels?** | The hook | Half the audience think the store is UK only, so they never buy |
| 3 | **Reach or sales?** | The lane, the sound, the length | Sales, because the whole point is removing an objection |
| 4 | **Which product is the honest answer?** | The bridge and the landing page | Starter Pack, because that is what is in the box going to Atlanta |
| 5 | **What are the first five words?** | The scroll stop | "Yes, we ship to America." |

Question 4 can come back "none". That is fine. Then it is a story or journey post and the CTA is follow or comment, not the link.

## The lanes
Every piece sits in one lane, decided at question 3, before filming.

**Reach lane. Wash and style under a sound.**
The franchise. Satisfying visuals, Scout's sound, durag pull on the early hit, reveal on the drop. Fifteen to twenty five seconds. Voiceover is one line at most, or none. This is where new people come from, including people who do not even wave.

**Sales lane. Voiceover or talking.**
Fewer views, more orders. Twenty to twenty six seconds, not fifty. The 14 Days to Miami Day 1 debrief is the evidence, retention died at twelve seconds on a fifty second cut. Honest open, the wave beat, the product as the answer to the problem the story just made real, one CTA.

**Third lane, long form.** If the raw material is a question people keep asking in the comments, it is not a TikTok, it is a Professor piece for YouTube. Hand it over.

A healthy week has both of the first two. Reach without sales is views. Sales without reach is talking to the same 190k.

## The output shape
**Reach cut (15 to 25s).** Hook on screen, lowercase, calm, first five seconds only. Durag pull on the first hit. Steps play. Reveal line, if there is one, lands on the drop. CTA lives in the caption, not the video.

**Sales cut (20 to 26s).** First five words carry the stake, no filler. Wave beat early. Three or four short beats, each ending on a small hook into the next. Product appears as the thing that solved it. One CTA. Close clean and forward, never a recap. "And as always, stay wavy man."

**Always deliver with the script:** the on screen hook line, the caption (one line echoing the hook, one line of value, soft CTA, three to six hashtags), and where the reveal lands if there is a sound.

### 2. "Made 40 jars of butter at 11pm after a shift"
Lane: sales. Product: Moisturising Butter. Film: the whipping, the jars lining up, the clock.

### 3. "Crown finally connected this morning"
Lane: reach. Product: soft nod to the butter and the hard brush in the caption only. Film: the wash and style, Scout's sound, durag pull on the first hit, the crown shown last.

## What kills it
Pulled from the learning logs and JR's own corrections. Any one of these and the post underperforms.
- Filler openers. "Real talk", "so", "okay so", "guys". The first five words carry the stake or they go.
- Fifty seconds. Twenty to twenty six for voiceover. Reach cuts shorter.
- Recap endings. Close forward, never list what happened.
- On screen text after the five second hook.
- "POV", emoji spam, forced tension. Lowercase and calm, or nothing.
- Stating exclusivity. The energy carries it, the words never do.
- The word "aura".
- Any technique line that is not in the canon. One wrong wave claim costs more than ten missed posts.
- Any mention of where the brushes come from.
- Dashes.

## Close the loop
The engine only works if the thing gets filmed and the result gets logged. Twelve weeks of nothing logged is the reason every rule in this folder is still a guess.
1. Say the film date out loud on Monday.
2. Drop the raw line. Get the script.
3. Film it. Post it.
4. Log it the same day: `node tools/post.mjs add --hook "..." --format talking --views N --watch S --result win --lesson "..."`
5. Tell The Voice how it did. That is what makes the next script better than this one.
