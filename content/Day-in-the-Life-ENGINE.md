# Day-in-the-Life ENGINE
_By The Content Plug for JR · The repeatable system: JR gives the day's plan → agent returns the full script._

## How JR uses this (the whole point)
JR just drops the day's plan in one line. The agent returns a ready-to-record **50–70s voiceover script** with on-screen text, wave content baked in, and the energy/exclusivity from the Miami series. No back-and-forth needed.

**What JR tells the agent:**
> "Today: [main thing/destination] · Stops: [e.g. food halfway, shops] · Gym? [yes = which Miami day / no] · Highlights: [anything special — restaurant, moment, who's there] · Names: [food spot / restaurant if known]"

**Then the agent ALWAYS asks one question back: "How's the swirl coming today?"** (JR's live 360 beehive → swirl pattern-shift journey). Whatever JR replies — e.g. "crown's connected, back's still behind" — gets woven into that day's script as a running thread. The swirl journey is part of the daily wave beat, so every day is also a chapter of the transformation. Everything else is engineered below.

## The DITL formula (50–70s, vlog-style VO)
Always this shape:

1. **Cold-open hook (0–3s)** — front-load the single most eye-catching moment of the whole day (or a bold tease of it). Never open on the boring start. On-screen text hits immediately.
2. **Wave beat + SWIRL update — MANDATORY every day** — a quick wave moment early (morning brush, durag on, "waves don't take days off") AND a line on how the 360 swirl is coming, based on JR's swirl-check reply. This is both the daily Wave Plug integration and an ongoing chapter of the pattern-shift series. Keep it natural; soft product nod only where it fits.
3. **Segment chain (3–5 beats)** — the actual day: travel → food stop → arrival/gym → evening. **End every segment with a micro-hook to the next** ("but the best bit came later," "then it got mad") so the 50–70s holds.
4. **Gym / Miami-series tie-in** — if there's a gym that day, fold in the workout as the series continuing ("even on holiday, prep doesn't stop — day X"). Ties the two series together.
5. **Exclusivity through ENERGY, not words** — never state it outright ("you won't get this anywhere else"). The confidence, quality and pace MAKE them feel it's one-of-one. Show, don't tell. ("This is how the plug moves" is fine — it's attitude, not a claim.)
6. **Close** — quick reflection + follow-driver tied to a payoff ("tomorrow's gonna be even better, follow so you don't miss it") + signature closer: "and as always… stay wavy man."

## On-screen text — HOOK ONLY (first ~5 seconds)
JR's rule: on-screen text appears **only for the first ~5 seconds** — purely the hook. After that, NO on-screen text; the voiceover and visuals carry the whole rest of the video.

**The hook test:** "if someone reads this, would they actually stop and watch?" If not, rewrite it.

**The vibe:** understated and premium, never clickbait. NO "POV," no emoji spam (one tired-style emoji like 😩 is fine), no forced tension. Quiet, cool, plainly stated. Dead labels ("road trip 🚗") are out.

**What makes a minimal hook stop the scroll:**
- Quiet tension/stakes, stated plainly — "didn't even get to brush my hair today 😩" · "waves don't get holidays."
- A small curiosity gap — "even the airbnb's got a gym."

**Rules:**
- ONE hook line, first ~5s only, lowercase, readable in a second.
- Don't repeat the spoken hook word-for-word.
- After 5s: no on-screen text at all.

## Retention rules for the longer format
- Pace stays fast — cut dead air, no lingering shots.
- Micro-hook between every scene (see step 3).
- On-screen text ONLY for the first ~5s hook (JR's preference) — none after.
- Keep a trending sound low under the voice.
- Best/most visual moment teased in the first 3 seconds, delivered later.

## Carried-over rules
- **Energy:** turned up, punchy, punch the CAPS words — not flat.
- **Voice:** still JR — calm-confident, UK slang natural, brotherly.
- **Wave content daily** — non-negotiable, it's the through-line.
- **Never use "aura."** Never state exclusivity outright — let the energy imply it.
- Guardrails: draft only, JR records/posts; keep Torino supplier confidential.
