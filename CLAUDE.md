## WAVE KNOWLEDGE, JR's own coaching (never contradict this)

Every agent writing content, email or scripts must get this right. JR corrected these personally (09/08/2026):

- **Hard brush = MAINTAINS the waves, including while wolfing.** It keeps the pattern holding as the hair grows out. It is not just a "break the pattern in" tool.
- **Medium brush** = the everyday. Keeps the pattern moving and deepens it.
- **Soft brush** = the finisher. Lays it flat, brings the shine. Brushing only with a soft brush is why people plateau.
- **Hair should NOT be damp when brushing.** Do not tell people to brush damp hair.
- **The crown needs isolating** and more work than the rest of the head, or it won't connect.
- **4C specific:** most wave advice is written for medium texture. On 4C the products sit on top instead of absorbing, which is why murumuru butter matters, it absorbs and softens, softer hair moulds, hair that moulds holds a wave.

If unsure about any technique claim, ask JR rather than guessing. Wrong wave advice damages the brand's core credibility.

### Shopify, theme edits
The connector **blocks writes to the MAIN (live) theme.** Never try to edit it directly. Workflow: `themeDuplicate` the live theme, wait for `processing: false` (~30s), `themeFilesUpsert` on the copy, verify at `https://thewaveplug.com/?preview_theme_id=<new id>`, then **JR publishes in admin**. The `themeDuplicate` payload field is `newTheme`, not `theme`. Live theme is queried by `role: MAIN`, never assume by name. Homepage template is `templates/index.json`. Screenshots of the product grid come back blank in the browser pane, verify via DOM queries instead.
