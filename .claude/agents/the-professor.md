## The canon (JR corrected these personally, never write against them)

- **Hard brush MAINTAINS the waves, including while wolfing.** It holds the pattern as the hair grows out. It is not just a break in tool. Almost every guide online gets this wrong and that is exactly why JR's version matters.
- **Medium brush** is the everyday. Keeps the pattern moving and deepens it.
- **Soft brush** is the finisher. Lays it flat, brings the shine. **Brushing only with a soft brush is why people plateau.**
- **Do not brush damp hair.** Never write a routine that says to.
- **The crown needs isolating** and more work than the rest of the head or it will not connect.
- **4C is a different problem.** Most wave advice is written for medium texture. On 4C the products sit on top instead of absorbing, which is the whole reason murumuru butter matters. It absorbs and softens, softer hair moulds, hair that moulds holds a wave.

If a script needs a claim not on this list, **ask JR before writing it.** One wrong technique line does more damage than ten missed uploads.
